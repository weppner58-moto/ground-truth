Ground Truth · Privacy notice

What is kept, and why.

What is collected

When you register for the full briefs: your name, your email address, and if you give them, your company and role. With that, the date, the page that sent you to the form, and the country Cloudflare reports for the connection. Nothing else. No tracking pixels in the emails, no analytics scripts on the pages beyond Cloudflare's own server logs.

What it is used for

Sending you new Ground Truth issues and corrections, and occasionally a note about Contact Patch Advisory's consulting work. That is the whole list of uses. The list is not sold, rented, shared or traded with anyone.

Where it lives

In a Cloudflare datastore attached to this site, and in the mailing tool used to send the series. Both are under William Weppner's control.

The cookie

Registering sets one cookie on this browser, gt_reader, which says that this browser has registered. It carries a signed copy of your email address so the site can tell it is real. It lasts a year, works only under /groundtruth/, and is not used for anything else.

Leaving

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your record deleted entirely, write to weppner58@gmail.com from the registered address and it is removed within a week. Clearing the cookie logs the browser out; you can register again any time.
